Controls and gameplay

Left / right: move
Up: rotate
Down: step down
Space: drop
P: pause
C: toggle Condor
1-9 / 0: launch arsenal slot

Clear dice to earn their pip values. Clearing multiple lines multiplies the funds. A smile is worth $150 only on the turn it lands.

First to top out loses. Pause stops both boards.

Offline matches open the bazaar every 20 combined lines. Queued attacks arrive at a piece boundary. Mirror makes the affected opponent's future attacks rebound; some are nullified.

Swap exchanges settled boards and replaces the peer's falling piece. Keating transfers funds or debt; Mondale shares earnings. Lawyers queues a rise for each peer-cleared line while active. Mirror nullifies Swap, Lazy Susan, Mondale, Keating, Have a Nice Day, and Mirror.

Fallout opens the middle six columns below the board; pieces can fall through without earning funds. Bottleneck narrows the middle of the board to four columns. Swap cancels Bottleneck on both boards.

A blocked descent gives 150 ms to slide or rotate; movement does not reset the timer. Slide Denied removes this grace. Hard drop always locks immediately.

Hatter rotates your piece every 20 ms. Slick moves it sideways every 20 ms and reverses at obstacles. Manual down stops Slick for the current piece; landing grace also stops its sideways movement.

Upbyside-down flips the board, spawns at the bottom, and reverses gravity and horizontal controls. Rotation stays the same. Swap restores normal gravity on both boards.

As in native Ernie matches, free Condor starts enabled; C toggles it. Reports show the settled board after placement, never the falling piece or arsenal. Ames reports about half the cells and unreliable funds; Ace reports about 85% and usually accurate funds; Condor is accurate.

About this browser port

Solo practice and offline Ernie matches include all 34 weapons. This release plays without sound. Matches stay in memory; reloading starts over. Ernie's movement and tactics are adapted from the original game. For private two-player matches, open online play on a BattleTris server.
