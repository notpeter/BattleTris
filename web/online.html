Controls and online play

Left / right: move. Up: rotate. Down: step down. Space: fast drop. P: pause or resume. 1-9 / 0: launch an arsenal slot.

Either player can pause or resume. Switching tabs pauses the match. A lost connection stops both boards temporarily while the player reconnects.

First to top out loses. The bazaar opens every 20 combined lines. Weapons arrive at a piece boundary. Human matches start without free reconnaissance; buy Ames, Ace, or Condor to receive reports.

The server runs the match. Controls wait for server updates, so network delay affects responsiveness. This release plays without sound.
