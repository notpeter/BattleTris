How to play

Build your board. Fund your arsenal. Outlast your opponent.

Game plan

Keep your stack low and leave room for dice and smiles. The first player to top out loses. Clear dice to earn their pip values; clearing two, three, or four lines multiplies the payout by that number. A smile earns $150 only if cleared on the turn it lands. Otherwise it becomes a frown.

Every 20 combined lines, both boards stop for the bazaar. Select a weapon, buy copies, and press Done when ready. Play resumes when both players finish. Ten arsenal slots hold stacks of matching weapons. Undo purchase refunds only copies bought during this visit.

Save for a strong attack or buy cheap disruptions. Flip out can spoil a setup; No Dice cuts income; Hatter and Weird combine well. Keep a Swap for emergencies. Launch a numbered arsenal slot during play. Attacks wait until a piece boundary. Durations below count lines cleared by the affected player; spies count the opponent's lines.

Controls

Left/right: move. Up: rotate. Down: step down. Space: fast drop. P: pause or resume. 1-9 and 0: launch slots 1-10. Touch buttons provide the same controls. Space starts fast descent rather than instant placement. A blocked piece normally has 150 ms to slide or rotate; movement does not restart that timer.

Against Ernie, free Condor starts enabled; C toggles it. Reconnaissance shows settled blocks, never the falling piece or arsenal, and cannot reveal invisible blocks. Human matches start without a spy. Either player can pause or resume online; a lost connection temporarily stops the match for reconnection.
